# CookUnity

> CookUnity is the first chef-to-consumer meal marketplace, connecting award-winning chefs with food lovers nationwide. Chefs craft restaurant-quality meals in local kitchens and deliver them fresh — ready to heat in 2 minutes. With 300+ rotating dishes, CookUnity offers unparalleled variety across cuisines and dietary options. Plans range from 4 to 16 meals per week, starting from $12.59/meal (50% off the first week). Not meal kits. Not frozen. Just fresh, chef-crafted food.
